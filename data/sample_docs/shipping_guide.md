# TechNova — Shipping Guide

*Last Updated: June 15, 2025*

## Domestic Shipping (United States)

### Standard Shipping
- **Cost**: Free on orders over $50 | $5.99 for orders under $50
- **Delivery Time**: 5-7 business days
- **Carrier**: USPS or UPS Ground
- **Tracking**: Provided via email within 24 hours of shipment

### Expedited Shipping
- **Cost**: $12.99
- **Delivery Time**: 2-3 business days
- **Carrier**: UPS 2nd Day Air
- **Tracking**: Real-time tracking with SMS updates
- **Cut-off**: Orders placed before 2PM EST ship same day

### Overnight Shipping
- **Cost**: $24.99
- **Delivery Time**: Next business day
- **Carrier**: UPS Next Day Air
- **Tracking**: Real-time tracking with SMS and email updates
- **Cut-off**: Orders placed before 12PM EST ship same day
- **Restrictions**: Not available for P.O. boxes or APO/FPO addresses

### Free Shipping Promotions
- All SmartHome Hub Pro and Enterprise orders ship free regardless of total
- TechNova Premium members get free expedited shipping on all orders
- Holiday promotions (Black Friday through Cyber Monday) include free overnight shipping

## International Shipping

### Available Regions

| Region | Shipping Cost | Delivery Time | Duties & Taxes |
|--------|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| Canada | $14.99 | 7-10 business days | Included (DDP) |
| UK & EU | $19.99 | 10-14 business days | Included (DDP) |
| Australia & NZ | $24.99 | 14-21 business days | NOT included (DDU) |
| Japan & South Korea | $19.99 | 10-14 business days | NOT included (DDU) |
| Rest of World | $29.99 | 14-28 business days | NOT included (DDU) |

**DDP** = Delivered Duty Paid (we pay import fees)
**DDU** = Delivered Duty Unpaid (you pay import fees at delivery)

### International Shipping Notes
- We ship from our fulfillment centers in **Reno, NV** and **Rotterdam, Netherlands**
- EU orders are shipped from Rotterdam to avoid additional customs delays
- Some products may have **export restrictions** — check product page for availability
- We do **not** ship to: Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, or Crimea region

## Order Processing

### Order Timeline
1. **Order Placed** — Confirmation email sent immediately
2. **Processing** (1-2 business days) — Payment verified, inventory allocated
3. **Quality Check** — Product inspected before shipping
4. **Shipped** — Tracking number provided via email and SMS
5. **In Transit** — Real-time tracking available at technova.com/track
6. **Delivered** — Delivery confirmation with photo proof (where available)

### Business Days
- Business days are **Monday through Friday**, excluding US federal holidays
- Orders placed on weekends or holidays begin processing the next business day
- During peak seasons (Nov-Dec), processing may take an additional 1-2 days

## Tracking Your Order

### How to Track
1. Visit [technova.com/track](https://technova.com/track)
2. Enter your **order number** or **tracking number**
3. View real-time location and estimated delivery date

### Tracking Statuses Explained
- **Label Created** — Shipping label printed, package not yet picked up
- **In Transit** — Package is moving through the carrier network
- **Out for Delivery** — Package will be delivered today
- **Delivered** — Package has been delivered (check mailbox, porch, or lobby)
- **Exception** — Delivery issue (weather delay, incorrect address, etc.)
- **Returned to Sender** — Undeliverable, package returning to TechNova

## Shipping Issues

### Package Not Received
If your tracking shows "Delivered" but you haven't received your package:
1. **Wait 24 hours** — sometimes tracking updates before actual delivery
2. **Check with neighbors** and around your property
3. **Check with building management** if you live in an apartment
4. Contact us within **7 days** of the delivery date and we'll:
   - File a claim with the carrier
   - Send a **replacement** or issue a **full refund** (your choice)
   - Typically resolved within 5 business days

### Incorrect Address
- You can update your shipping address within **1 hour** of placing your order
- After 1 hour, contact support immediately — we may be able to intercept the package
- If the package is delivered to the wrong address, we'll send a replacement at no charge

### Damaged Package
See our [Refund Policy](refund_policy.md) for instructions on damaged shipments.

## Signature Requirements
- Orders over **$200** require a signature at delivery
- You can authorize "signature not required" through your account settings
- If you're not available, the carrier will make **3 delivery attempts**
- After 3 failed attempts, the package is held at the nearest carrier location for 7 days
