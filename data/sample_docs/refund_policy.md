# TechNova — Refund & Return Policy

*Last Updated: July 1, 2025*

## Overview

At TechNova, we stand behind the quality of our products. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help. This policy covers all products purchased directly from TechNova (technova.com) or authorized retail partners.

## 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

All TechNova products come with a **30-day money-back guarantee** from the date of delivery. During this period:

- You may return the product for **any reason** — no questions asked
- We will issue a **full refund** including the original shipping cost
- The product must be in its **original packaging** with all accessories included
- Products must be in **resellable condition** (no physical damage beyond normal unboxing)

### How to Request a Refund

1. **Log in** to your TechNova account at [account.technova.com](https://account.technova.com)
2. Navigate to **Orders** > select the order > click **Request Return**
3. Choose your reason (optional) and select **Refund** as the resolution
4. Print the **prepaid return shipping label** (US customers only)
5. Pack the product securely and drop it off at any UPS location
6. Refund is processed within **5-7 business days** after we receive the item

### International Returns
For orders shipped outside the United States:
- Return shipping costs are the **customer's responsibility**
- Customs duties and import taxes are **non-refundable**
- Processing time may be extended to **10-14 business days**
- Contact support@technova.com for a return authorization number before shipping

## Extended Warranty Returns (31-365 days)

After the 30-day window but within **one year** of purchase:

- Returns are accepted for **defective products only**
- You must provide proof of the defect (photo or video)
- We will offer either a **replacement** or **store credit** (your choice)
- Refunds to original payment method are available at our discretion
- All warranty claims require the **original receipt** or order confirmation

### What's Covered Under Warranty
- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware malfunctions not caused by user damage
- Battery degradation below 80% capacity
- Software bugs that render the device non-functional

### What's NOT Covered
- Physical damage (drops, water damage, crushing)
- Damage from unauthorized modifications or repairs
- Normal wear and tear
- Cosmetic damage that doesn't affect functionality
- Damage from power surges (use a surge protector!)

## Exceptions

### Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned:
- **Gift cards** — non-refundable, non-transferable
- **Customized/engraved products** — final sale
- **Software licenses** — non-refundable once activated
- **Clearance items** — marked as "Final Sale" at checkout

### Damaged During Shipping
If your product arrives damaged:
1. **Do not discard** the packaging or product
2. Take **photos** of the damage (packaging and product)
3. Contact us within **48 hours** of delivery
4. We will send a **free replacement** via expedited shipping
5. A carrier will pick up the damaged item at no cost to you
